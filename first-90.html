The First 90 Days After an Autism Diagnosis

The first 90 days are not about fixing autism.

They are about stabilizing your infrastructure.

  1. Write down the three moments of your diagnosis.
    Suspicion. The word. Lifelong.
  2. Name the blueprint you were carrying.
    Say what you imagined. Say what is still possible.
  3. Start the first team.
    One person who will sit with you in the next meeting.
  4. Begin the records.
    One folder. Every evaluation. Every plan. Every promise in writing.
  5. Ask the question early.
    What does independence look like for him?

The diagnosis is not the tragedy. Unpreparedness is.
